DRIVEHUB GH — FLEET PAGE UPDATE

Included:
- fleet.html
- styles.css
- script.js
- assets/fleet/ (five optimized WebP model visuals)

Install:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload/replace fleet.html, styles.css, and script.js in the root of the existing DriveHub-GH repository.
3. Upload the included assets/fleet folder into the existing assets folder. Do not delete other assets.
4. Commit to main and wait for Cloudflare Pages to deploy.
5. Open /fleet and hard-refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R.

Notes:
- Fleet cards now feature Toyota Prado, Mitsubishi Outlander (2018–2023 model range), Toyota RAV4 (2018–2023 model range), Hyundai Santa Fe, and Honda CR-V.
- Model cards link to Find Your Ride with the selected model carried into the enquiry summary.
- The visuals are polished illustrative model renders, not verified photographs of individual units. The page includes a short disclosure so customers know exact colour, trim, year and availability are confirmed with their enquiry.
- No rates, seating capacity, luggage capacity, or live availability are invented.
