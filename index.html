Do you offer self-drive rentals? +

Yes. DriveHub GH is a self-drive car rental service. We rent out vehicles for you to drive yourself; chauffeur services are not offered.

How do I find out the rental price? +

Send us your dates and preferred vehicle. We’ll confirm availability and share the applicable rate and rental terms before you commit.

What documents do I need? +

Requirements can depend on your circumstances and the vehicle. Contact us before booking so we can confirm the documents and eligibility requirements.

Can I extend my rental? +

Extensions are subject to availability and approval. Please contact us before your scheduled return time to discuss options and any additional charges.

Where is pickup? +

Our office is at 22 Baiden Avenue, 2nd Manu Street, Westland, Accra, Ghana. Please confirm your pickup arrangements with our team.
